Pre-Release

EZ Minutes is still in testing. Any and all feedback is welcome — please use with caution, and keep a backup of anything important. Reach the developer, Kevin of SingLoud.org, at sacredharpbiblio@gmail.com.

Before You Begin

Use a laptop, desktop computer, or tablet with a physical keyboard. EZ Minutes is not designed for phones. Capture works offline. Compile uses the CSV file saved from Capture.
